Zivra: AI Health Tracker

Privacy Policy. Effective date: 12 November 2026.

This policy explains what information the Zivra app ("Zivra", "we") handles. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

1. Your health entries stay on your phone

The things you log in Zivra (sleep, water, mood, weight, workouts) and the insights and charts built from them are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to our servers and we cannot see them. If you uninstall the app or use "Delete my data", they are removed from your device.

2. What we do receive

  • Anonymous app ID. The first time you open the app, it creates a random ID on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or contact list. We use it to recognise your subscription and, if you send feedback, to connect that feedback to a deletion request.
  • Subscription information. Purchases are handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat (a subscription management service) to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your anonymous app ID and purchase details from Google Play (for example product, price, and renewal status). We never see your payment card or bank details.
  • Feedback you choose to send. If you open the feedback screen and press send, we receive your anonymous app ID, your plan status, your app language, the options you ticked, and any text you typed. Please do not put personal or medical details in this text. Feedback is stored on Cloudflare servers that we operate.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, camera, microphone or contacts.

4. Deleting your data

In the app, open Profile and choose "Delete my data". This erases your entries on your phone and also deletes the feedback stored under your anonymous app ID on our server. You can also email us at the address below and we will delete anything we hold about you. Subscription records held by Google Play and RevenueCat follow their own policies; to cancel a subscription, use Google Play, Subscriptions.

5. Not medical advice

Zivra is a tracking and wellness tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and does not replace a doctor. If you feel unwell, please see a medical professional.

6. Children

Zivra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Future features

We plan to add an AI coach later. Before it launches, we will update this policy to explain what it processes and ask for your consent where required.

8. Security

Data sent to our server travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No system is perfectly secure, but we collect as little as possible so there is little to lose.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Material changes will also be shown in the app.
